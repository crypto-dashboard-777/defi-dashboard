How this is computed: federal and NY State income tax use 2024 single‑filer brackets and standard deductions; payroll tax applies 6.2% Social Security to the wage base plus 1.45% Medicare (0.9% additional above $200k). NYC tax, if toggled, is layered on the same NY taxable base. The pain specialist's income ramps linearly from her starting figure to the year‑10 figure, then grows at the shared raise rate; the overhead slider is an extra haircut for practice costs — malpractice, staff, self‑employment tax — not already reflected in the salary figures. Ignored: investment gains tax, retirement‑account sheltering, geographic salary variance, loan payments, and the non‑financial side of the decision — career satisfaction, patient relationships, autonomy — which this was never going to capture.
