What We Build

Every Situation.
Every Structure.

We work with what you have — an apartment, an existing building, or open land. We have built in all of these situations before.

Safe Rooms Inside Apartments

A blast-resistant room built inside your existing apartment. We reinforce walls, ceiling, and the door frame — without requiring you to move or demolish.

Ground-Floor Shelter Construction

Blast-resistant shelters built on the ground floor of residential or commercial buildings. We assess the existing structure and build around it.

Underground Bunkers

Full underground shelter construction on open land or beneath existing buildings — reinforced concrete, blast doors, ventilation, power, and water included.

Retrofitting Existing Buildings

We strengthen what is already there. Basements, stairwells, storage rooms — we evaluate the space and engineer it into a compliant, liveable shelter.

Community & Multi-Unit Shelters

Shared shelters for apartment buildings, gated communities, or office complexes. Designed for capacity, fast access, and full life-support systems.

Government & Large-Scale Facilities

Hardened facilities for government, defence, and critical infrastructure — full project documentation, engineering oversight, and construction supervision.
